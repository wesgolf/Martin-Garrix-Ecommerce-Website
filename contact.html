SHIPPING & RETURNS.

01

Dispatch

Orders are usually packed within 1–2 working days. You will receive a tracking link as soon as your parcel leaves the warehouse.

02

Worldwide delivery

Delivery times vary by destination. Local duties or import taxes may be collected by the carrier when your parcel arrives.

03

30-day returns

Unworn, unwashed pieces with their original tags can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

Need-to-know

FREQUENTLY
ASKED.

Where is my order?

Once your parcel is dispatched, its tracking link will appear in your shipping email. Allow up to 24 hours for the first carrier update.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Send us a message as soon as possible. We cannot guarantee changes after an order enters packing, but we will always check.

How should I choose a size?

Our apparel uses a relaxed unisex fit. Open the size guide on each product page for fit notes before adding your size to the bag.

Is this a real store?

This website is a front-end ecommerce concept. Its forms demonstrate the experience and do not send support requests or create real orders.
